## P2P "P2PEscrowV2: invalid trade amount" when registering a trade on-chain
Cause: offer e9acb3c8 was cancelled + its 50 USDC withdrawn on-chain on 2026-09-28 17:58 UTC (p2p_refund activity),
but the DB update marking it cancelled was blocked by the broken RLS policy (fixed by 20260929000000), so the offer
stayed 'active' with escrow_balance 50 while on-chain remaining = 0. registerTrade then reverted.
Data repair applied live: that offer -> cancelled, escrow_balance 0, escrow_withdraw_tx_hash set.
Trade 9062ccdb (open dispute, admin_frozen) left untouched - it needs an admin to resolve.
src/lib/p2pService.ts: confirmSellTradeOnChain now shows a plain-language message for this revert.

## History receipt popup flicker (Android Chrome)
src/components/ui/ReceiptPopup.tsx + src/index.css: while a receipt popup is open, every backdrop-filter beneath it
(sticky page headers, cards, nav) is disabled via html.receipt-open, and the animated card gets its own compositor layer
(willChange / backface-visibility). The earlier fix only removed the blur on the popup's own backdrop; the page headers
under it kept re-blurring on every animation frame. NOT verified on a device - please test on Android Chrome.
